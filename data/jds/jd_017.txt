About the role

* Develop and execute Finku's organic social media strategy.

Paid Marketing

* Support the management and optimization of Meta, TikTok, and Google Ads campaigns.

* Deep understanding of Gen Z and Millennial audiences and digital culture.

* Creative, analytical, and highly experimental.
