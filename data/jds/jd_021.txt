About the role
About the Role
erad is a Sharia-compliant fintech providing SME financing in Saudi Arabia and the UAE, licensed by the CMA in KSA and the DFSA in the UAE. We are hiring a Cybersecurity Engineer to own the security of our platform, our people and our devices. You will report to the CTO and work with a fully distributed engineering team that ships with AI-assisted tooling every day. We especially want to hear from penetration testers and security auditors who are ready to move from finding the issues to owning the fixes.

What You'll Do
Security engineering and testing

Own the security posture of our cloud environment: identity and access, network exposure, host hardening and patching, secrets, and logging coverage.
Test our own products the way an attacker would: regular internal pentests of our web apps and APIs, plus scoping and retests with external testers.
Build and run vulnerability management across code, dependencies, hosts and third-party software, with remediation SLAs tracked to closure.
Review designs and pull requests with engineers, and build security into our delivery pipeline.
Detection and response

Own security alerting and keep it useful rather than noisy.
Lead incident response end to end: triage, containment, investigation, root cause and the write-up for management and regulators.
Run phishing awareness, tabletop exercises and restore tests.
Governance and compliance

Run our ISMS: control evidence, risk register, policies, vendor security reviews, access reviews and audit support.
Turn regulatory requirements (CMA, DFSA, and KSA and UAE data protection law) into practical controls and evidence.
IT (around 20% of your time)

Run joiner, mover and leaver access: accounts, SSO, app roles, offboarding and data handover.
Manage company laptops through MDM: enrolment, configuration, encryption and app deployment.
Administer our identity and collaboration tools, the company password manager and MFA.
Automate the repetitive parts so IT stays a small slice of the job.

Practical security experience on at least one major cloud provider: IAM, network controls and logging.

Strong Linux and networking fundamentals: SSH, TLS, DNS, firewalls and VPNs.
